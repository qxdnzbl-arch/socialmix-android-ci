KeepExact

Check whether an AI changed only what you asked.

KeepExact is a checker, not an image editor. Use it after you edit an image with any AI tool. It tells you whether your requested change was completed and whether the AI also changed something you told it to preserve.

What it catches

Example

You asked: “Remove the cup. Keep the face and background unchanged.” The cup is gone, but the face also changed. KeepExact should flag that extra change.

Use it for

Object removal, color changes, local replacements, and other AI edits where some parts must stay unchanged.

What it does not do

It does not generate or repair images. It only checks the before/after images against your original instruction.
